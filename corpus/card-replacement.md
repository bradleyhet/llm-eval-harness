# Replacing your card

This page covers what to do when your Corella Card is lost, stolen or damaged, and how replacement cards are delivered.

## Lost card

If you cannot find your card, freeze it first from the card screen. A frozen card cannot be used but can be unfrozen if it turns up. If you have not found it within a day or two, report it as lost in the app. Reporting a card lost cancels it permanently and orders a replacement. A replacement for a lost card is free with standard delivery. You will not be held liable for transactions made after the moment you froze or reported the card.

## Stolen card

If you believe your card has been stolen, report it as stolen in the app or call 1300 000 267, which is open 24 hours a day for this purpose. A stolen card is cancelled immediately and cannot be reinstated. The app will show you recent transactions so you can flag any you did not make, and each flagged transaction is lodged as an unauthorised transaction dispute automatically. A replacement for a stolen card is free with standard delivery. You may be asked for a police report number if unauthorised transactions total more than $1,000.

## Damaged card

A card that no longer taps or reads in a terminal can be replaced from the card screen by choosing damaged. The old card keeps working until the new one is activated, so you are not left without a card. The first replacement for a damaged card in any 12 month period is free. Further replacements for damage in the same 12 months cost $10 each.

## Standard replacement

Standard replacement cards are posted to your residential address or nominated post office box and arrive within five to seven business days. Standard replacement is free for lost, stolen and first damaged cards. Your virtual card keeps working throughout, so you can still pay with your phone wallet while you wait. When the new card arrives, activate it in the app. The card number changes with every replacement, so update any merchants that hold your physical card details.

## Express replacement

If you need a new card urgently, express replacement is the faster way to get one sent out. Express replacement costs $15 and is delivered by courier. Cards sent express arrive within two business days to metropolitan addresses and within three business days to regional addresses. Express replacement is available for lost, stolen and damaged cards, and the $15 fee is charged to your Everyday account when you place the order. Express delivery requires a residential street address; it cannot be sent to a post office box.
