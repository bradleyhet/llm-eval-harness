# Security and two factor approval

Corella Bank protects your account with a passcode, biometrics and two factor approval for sensitive actions. This page describes each layer.

## App passcode and biometrics

Your app passcode is six digits and is set when you open your account. You can unlock the app with your fingerprint or face instead of the passcode if your phone supports it, but the passcode is always required after a restart of the phone, after five failed biometric attempts, and when you approve high risk actions. Choose a passcode that is not your birth date or a repeated digit. You can change the passcode at any time from the security screen.

## Two factor approval

Two factor approval means confirming a sensitive action on your registered device in addition to being signed in. In the Corella app the second factor is a push notification that you approve with your passcode or biometrics. Two factor approval is required to raise a transfer or card limit, add a new payee and send them more than $1,000 on the same day, change your registered mobile number, turn off a card block, sign in on a new device, and request an international transfer limit increase.

## Text message codes

Text message codes are used as a fallback only, for example when you are setting up the app on a new phone and cannot yet receive push notifications. A code sent by text is valid for ten minutes and can be used once. Corella Bank staff will never ask you to read out a text message code, and you should never share one with anyone, including someone claiming to be from Corella Bank.

## New device sign in

When you sign in on a device Corella Bank has not seen before, you must approve the sign in from your existing device, or if you no longer have it, complete a text message code and a fresh liveness selfie. The new device is then trusted and your old device is signed out. You can see every trusted device on the security screen and remove any of them, which signs that device out immediately.

## Keeping your account safe

Corella Bank will never phone, text or chat to ask for your passcode, card PIN, text message code or full card number. Genuine Corella Bank messages appear inside the app's message centre, and any text message from Corella Bank will only ever direct you to open the app, never to a link. If you receive a message asking for security details, do not respond, and report it through the report a scam option in the app.
