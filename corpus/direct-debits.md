# Direct debits

A direct debit is an arrangement where a merchant or service takes payments from your Everyday account using your BSB and account number. This page explains how to set up, view, cancel and dispute direct debits.

## Setting up a direct debit

To set up a direct debit you give the merchant your BSB and account number, which are shown in the app under your Everyday account. The merchant then submits each payment request to Corella Bank. Direct debits can only be set up on an Everyday or Joint Everyday account, not on a Saver. Merchants can also charge your Corella Card on a recurring basis; that is a card subscription rather than a direct debit and is managed from the card screen.

## Viewing your direct debits

The app lists every merchant that has debited your account in the last 13 months under the direct debits screen, with the last amount and date. New direct debits appear after the first payment. You receive a push notification the first time a new merchant debits your account so you can check it is one you expected. You can also set a per merchant alert to notify you when a debit is higher than the previous one.

## Cancelling a direct debit

You can cancel any direct debit from the direct debits screen. Corella Bank blocks further requests from that merchant within one business day of your cancellation. You should also tell the merchant, because cancelling the debit does not cancel any contract you have with them, and they may pursue the payment another way. A cancelled direct debit can be reinstated only by setting it up again with the merchant.

## Dishonour fee

If a bill comes out by direct debit and there is no money, or not enough money, in your Everyday account to pay it, the payment is dishonoured and a dishonour fee of $7.50 is charged to your account. The merchant may also charge you their own failed payment fee. To avoid dishonours you can turn on low balance alerts, which notify you when your balance drops below an amount you choose, or set up an automatic top up from a Saver account on the day before a scheduled debit.

## Cancellation fee

There is no cancellation fee for direct debits. Cancelling a direct debit in the app is free regardless of how many you cancel or how long the arrangement has been in place. Corella Bank also does not charge a fee to reinstate a direct debit, because reinstatement is done by the merchant. The only direct debit related fee is the $7.50 dishonour fee for a payment that fails because of insufficient funds.

## Disputing an unauthorised direct debit

If a merchant debits your account without your authority, or takes an amount different from what you agreed, you can lodge a direct debit dispute from the direct debits screen within 90 days of the debit date. Corella Bank returns the disputed amount to your account within five business days of receiving the dispute and recovers it from the merchant's bank. This 90 day window is longer than the 60 day window for card transaction disputes.
