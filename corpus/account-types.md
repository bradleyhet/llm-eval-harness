# Account types

Corella Bank offers three transaction and savings products for personal customers: the Everyday account, the Saver account and the Joint Everyday account. All three are opened and managed entirely in the Corella app.

## Everyday account

The Everyday account is the main transaction account. It comes with a Corella Card, instant transfers to other Australian accounts, direct debit support and cash access through the Corella Cash Network. The Everyday account carries a monthly fee of $5, which is waived in any calendar month where you deposit $1,000 or more into the account. Salary, government payments and transfers from other banks all count towards the $1,000. The Everyday account does not pay interest on its balance. You can hold one Everyday account in your own name.

## Saver account

The Saver account is for putting money aside. It has no monthly fee and no card. Money moves in and out of a Saver only through a linked Everyday account, so you cannot spend from a Saver directly. The Saver pays a base variable rate of 0.10% per year on the whole balance. In any month where you deposit at least $200 and make no withdrawals, the bonus rate applies instead and you earn 4.35% per year for that month. Interest is calculated daily and paid on the last day of each month. You can open up to five Saver accounts and give each one a name and a savings goal.

## Joint Everyday account

The Joint Everyday account is an Everyday account shared by two people. Both holders must already be verified Corella Bank customers before the account can be opened. Each holder gets their own Corella Card linked to the joint account and their own app login. The account carries a single monthly fee of $5, shared between the holders and waived by the same $1,000 monthly deposit rule as a sole Everyday account. Joint accounts can be set to either-to-sign, where each holder can act alone, or both-to-sign for transfers over $2,000. There is no joint version of the Saver account.

## Who can open an account

To open any Corella Bank account you must be at least 16 years old, an Australian resident with a residential address in Australia, and hold an Australian mobile number. You need to pass identity verification in the app before the account is fully active. Customers aged 16 and 17 can open Everyday and Saver accounts in their own name without a parent or guardian. Accounts are for personal use only.

## Getting help

Support is available through in-app chat from 7am to 11pm Australian Eastern Standard Time every day of the year. You can also phone 1300 000 267. The phone line is open 24 hours a day for lost or stolen cards and for reporting scams, and from 8am to 8pm Australian Eastern Standard Time for everything else. Corella Bank does not offer email support and has no branches.
