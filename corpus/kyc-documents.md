# Identity documents

Corella Bank must verify who you are before your account is fully active. This page lists which documents are accepted and how they are checked.

## Primary photo identification

One primary photo document is required for every new customer. Accepted primary documents are an Australian driver licence, an Australian or foreign passport that has not expired, or an Australian proof of age card issued by a state or territory. The document must be physically in your possession when you sign up, because the app photographs the front and back and reads the security features. Photocopies, screenshots and expired documents are rejected automatically.

## Secondary documents

If the primary document alone does not confirm your identity, the app will ask for one secondary document. Accepted secondary documents are a Medicare card, an Australian birth certificate, an Australian citizenship certificate, or a utility bill or council rates notice dated within the last three months showing your name and residential address. Only one secondary document is ever needed. You will be asked for it in the app, and you can upload it as a photo or a PDF.

## Name and address matching

The name and date of birth you type in must match the primary document exactly, including middle names. If your current address is different from the one on your driver licence, you can still use the licence as primary identification, but you will be asked for a secondary document that shows your current address. Post office boxes are not accepted as a residential address, although you can nominate a post office box separately for mailed items such as paper statements.

## Documents for customers aged 16 and 17

Customers who are 16 or 17 can verify with the same documents as adults. A learner driver permit counts as a driver licence for this purpose. If you have no photo identification at all, an Australian birth certificate plus a Medicare card together are accepted as an alternative, and a specialist will review the application manually within two business days.

## Keeping your details current

You are required to keep your name, address and mobile number up to date. You can change your address and mobile number in the app at any time. Changing your mobile number requires two factor approval from your existing device. A change of legal name requires a fresh primary document in the new name, uploaded through the app, and takes up to two business days to process.
