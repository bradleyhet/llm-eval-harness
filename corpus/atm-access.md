# ATM access and cash

Corella Bank has no branches, so cash comes in and out through ATMs. This page explains where you can use your Corella Card for cash and what it costs.

## Corella Cash Network ATMs

Corella Bank partners with a group of ATM operators known as the Corella Cash Network. There are around 3,200 network ATMs across Australia, and they are marked on the map in the app. Withdrawals at network ATMs are free, with no charge from Corella Bank and no charge from the operator. You can find the nearest network ATM by opening the cash screen in the app, which shows ATMs within walking distance and whether each one accepts deposits.

## Other Australian ATMs

You can use any other ATM in Australia that accepts the card network. Withdrawals at these ATMs cost $2.50 each, charged by Corella Bank. Corella Bank rebates up to three of these fees per calendar month, credited to your account on the first day of the following month. Some ATM operators also charge their own fee, which is shown on the screen before you confirm; Corella Bank does not rebate operator fees.

## Overseas ATMs

You can withdraw local currency at ATMs overseas wherever the card network is accepted. Each overseas withdrawal costs $5, and the amount is converted at the mid market rate plus a margin of 0.5%. Some overseas operators charge their own fee. If the ATM offers to convert the withdrawal to Australian dollars for you, decline and choose to be charged in local currency, because the operator's conversion rate is usually worse than Corella Bank's. Overseas ATM access can be switched off in card controls.

## Daily withdrawal limit

The daily ATM withdrawal limit is $1,000 per customer, counted across all ATMs and both your physical and virtual card. The day resets at midnight Australian Eastern Standard Time. You can lower the limit in the app. The limit cannot be raised above $1,000, even with two factor approval. Cash withdrawals count separately from the $5,000 daily purchase limit, so reaching one limit does not affect the other.

## Cardless withdrawals

At Corella Cash Network ATMs you can withdraw cash without your card. Choose cardless withdrawal in the app, enter the amount, and the app shows a code that is valid for 30 minutes. At the ATM choose cardless, enter the code and your PIN, and take your cash. Cardless withdrawals count against the same $1,000 daily limit. This is useful if you are waiting for a replacement card.

## Depositing cash

To put notes and coins into your account, use a Corella Cash Network ATM marked as deposit capable on the map; cash deposits are accepted there and nowhere else. Deposits are free and are credited to your Everyday account immediately. The daily cash deposit limit is $3,000 per customer. Cash deposits count towards the $1,000 monthly deposit that waives the Everyday account fee. Cheques cannot be deposited at any ATM and Corella Bank does not accept cheques at all.
