# Reporting a scam

If you think you have been scammed, or have been contacted by someone pretending to be Corella Bank, act quickly. This page explains what to do and what Corella Bank can do to help.

## Scams versus unauthorised transactions

A scam is where you were tricked into sending money yourself, for example by a fake investment offer, a fake invoice, or someone impersonating a family member. An unauthorised transaction is where someone else used your card or account without your permission. The distinction matters because unauthorised transactions are handled as disputes with provisional credit, whereas scam payments you authorised are handled through the recall process described here. If you are unsure which applies, report it as a scam and the team will work it out with you.

## How to report a scam

Report a scam immediately by calling 1300 000 267, which is open 24 hours a day for scam reports, or by choosing report a scam from the help screen in the app. Have ready the payee details, the amount, the time of the payment and any messages you received. Reporting quickly matters because the chance of recovering money drops sharply after the first few hours. You do not need a police report to start a scam report.

## What Corella Bank does next

When you report a scam payment, Corella Bank immediately attempts to recall the funds from the receiving bank and asks that bank to freeze the account. For domestic transfers this request is sent within minutes. The team also checks your account for other suspicious activity, offers to freeze your card, and may reset your trusted devices. You receive an initial response with a case reference within two business days, and updates as the recall progresses. Recovery is not guaranteed, because the money may already have been moved on.

## Reimbursement

Corella Bank assesses each scam case individually. Where Corella Bank's own systems or staff failed to act on a clear warning sign, Corella Bank will reimburse the loss. Where the payment was authorised by you and Corella Bank warned you or had no reason to intervene, reimbursement is not automatic, but any recovered funds are returned to you in full. The assessment outcome is given in writing in the app within 30 days of the report.

## Reporting impersonation attempts

If someone contacts you claiming to be from Corella Bank and asks for a passcode, card PIN, text message code or card number, or asks you to move money to a safe account, it is a scam. Corella Bank never asks for those things and does not have safe accounts. Hang up or stop replying, and forward any text or email through the report a scam option in the app so the team can warn other customers.
