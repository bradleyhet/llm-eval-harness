# Disputing a transaction

If a card transaction on your account is wrong, you can lodge a dispute from the app. This page explains the two kinds of dispute, how to lodge one and what happens next.

## Unauthorised transactions

An unauthorised transaction is one you did not make and did not permit anyone else to make. Examples are purchases after your card was stolen or a charge from a merchant you have never dealt with. Before lodging, check whether the charge is a merchant using a different trading name, a family member with access to your card, or a subscription you forgot. If you are sure the transaction is unauthorised, freeze or cancel your card first, then lodge the dispute.

## Merchant disputes

A merchant dispute is where you did make the transaction but something went wrong: you did not receive the goods or service, you received something materially different from what was described, you were charged twice, you were charged the wrong amount, or you cancelled a subscription and were charged anyway. For a merchant dispute you must first contact the merchant and give them a reasonable chance to fix the problem. The app asks you to describe what you did and to attach any receipts or emails.

## When to lodge a dispute

A card transaction dispute must be lodged within 60 days of the transaction date. The 60 days is counted from the date the transaction was made, not the date it settled or the date you noticed it. Disputes lodged after 60 days are usually refused by the card network, although Corella Bank will still review them for unauthorised transactions. Disputes for unauthorised direct debits follow a different rule and can be lodged within 90 days. In plain terms: if you want your money back on a card charge you do not recognise or a dodgy transaction, the cut off for raising it is 60 days.

## How to lodge a dispute

Open the transaction in the app, choose dispute this transaction, and answer the questions. You will be asked whether the transaction was unauthorised or a merchant dispute, what happened, and for any supporting evidence. Lodging takes a few minutes and you receive a dispute reference immediately. You can add more evidence later from the dispute screen. For an unauthorised transaction on a stolen card you may be asked for a police report number if the total exceeds $1,000.

## Provisional credit

For unauthorised transactions, Corella Bank pays a provisional credit for the disputed amount within five business days of lodgement where the amount is under $500. For amounts of $500 or more, provisional credit is paid once the initial investigation confirms the claim is likely to succeed, which takes up to ten business days. If the dispute is later refused, the provisional credit is reversed and you are told why. Merchant disputes do not receive provisional credit; the refund is paid when the dispute is upheld.
