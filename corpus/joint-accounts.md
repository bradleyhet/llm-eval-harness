# Joint accounts

A Joint Everyday account is shared by two people. This page explains how to open one, how signing rules work, and what happens when a holder wants to leave.

## Opening a joint account

If you and your partner want to share one account, you open a Joint Everyday account. Both people must already be verified Corella Bank customers with their own Everyday account before a Joint Everyday account can be opened. One person starts the joint account from the accounts screen and invites the other by mobile number. The invitation is accepted in the second person's app with two factor approval. The joint account opens immediately after acceptance. A joint account can have exactly two holders; there is no option for three or more, and there is no joint version of the Saver.

## Signing rules

A joint account is either-to-sign by default, which means either holder can make transfers, set up direct debits and change controls without the other's approval. You can switch the account to both-to-sign for transfers over $2,000, so that any transfer above that amount needs approval from both holders in their own apps before it is sent. Changing the signing rule requires approval from both holders. Card purchases are never subject to both-to-sign.

## Cards and fees on a joint account

Each holder receives their own Corella Card, physical and virtual, linked to the joint account, with their own PIN and card controls. The joint account carries a single monthly fee of $5, waived in any month where $1,000 or more is deposited into the joint account from any source. Each holder's own Everyday account still has its own fee and its own waiver. Direct debits on a joint account are visible to both holders.

## Visibility and notifications

Both holders see every transaction, direct debit and statement for the joint account in their own app. Both receive push notifications for transactions on the joint account, although each holder controls their own notification threshold. Card transactions show which holder's card was used. Either holder can freeze their own card, but freezing one holder's card does not affect the other's.

## Removing a holder or closing a joint account

A holder cannot be removed from a joint account unilaterally. To remove a holder, both holders must approve the change in their apps, after which the account continues as a sole Everyday account of the remaining holder. To close the joint account entirely, one holder requests closure and the other has seven days to approve in their app; if they do not, the request lapses. The balance is split according to instructions both holders confirm, and closure is processed within two business days of the second approval.
