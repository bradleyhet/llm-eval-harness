# Card controls

Card controls let you decide where and how your Corella Card can be used. All controls are in the card screen of the app and take effect the moment you change them.

## Freezing and unfreezing

Freezing your card blocks every new transaction on both the physical and virtual card until you unfreeze it. Direct debits and existing subscriptions charged to the card are also declined while frozen. Freezing is instant and free, and you can freeze and unfreeze as often as you like. Use freeze when you have misplaced your card and expect to find it. If you are sure the card is gone, report it lost or stolen instead so a replacement can be sent.

## Blocking transaction types

You can switch off online purchases, overseas purchases, gambling transactions, ATM withdrawals and contactless payments independently. Each block applies to both the physical and virtual card. A blocked transaction is declined at the point of sale and you receive a push notification explaining which block caused the decline. Blocks stay on until you turn them off. Turning a block off requires two factor approval.

## Setting a lower purchase limit

The default daily purchase limit is $5,000. You can set a lower daily limit, for example $500, and the card will decline any purchase that would take the day's spending above that amount. The day resets at midnight Australian Eastern Standard Time. You can also set a per transaction limit, which declines any single purchase above the amount you choose. Raising a limit back towards the $5,000 maximum requires two factor approval.

## Merchant locks

Merchant lock lets you block a specific merchant from charging your card again. Open the transaction, choose lock merchant, and future attempts by that merchant are declined. This is useful for a subscription you have tried to cancel. A merchant lock does not reverse charges that have already been made. To recover a charge you did not authorise, lodge a dispute instead.

## Notifications

By default you receive a push notification for every transaction on your card, including declines. You can raise the notification threshold so that only transactions above a chosen amount notify you, but Corella Bank recommends leaving instant notifications on so you can spot unauthorised use quickly. Notifications for declined transactions cannot be turned off, and neither can notifications for a new merchant charging your card for the first time.
