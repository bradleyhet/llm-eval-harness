# Foreign exchange

This page explains how Corella Bank converts currency for card purchases and international transfers, and how to keep track of exchange rates.

## How the rate is set

Corella Bank converts currency at the mid market rate, which is the midpoint between the buy and sell rates on the wholesale currency market, plus a margin of 0.5%. The same margin applies whether you are paying with your Corella Card overseas, making an online purchase in a foreign currency, or sending an international transfer. Corella Bank does not add a separate international transaction fee on card purchases. The rate is updated continuously during the week.

## Weekend and public holiday rates

Wholesale currency markets close over the weekend. From Saturday morning to Monday morning Australian Eastern Standard Time, and on some international public holidays, Corella Bank uses the last available mid market rate plus the same 0.5% margin. There is no additional weekend surcharge. Card purchases made on a weekend are converted at the rate current when the merchant settles the transaction, which may be a business day later.

## Rate lock on transfers

When you set up an international transfer, the app shows the exact rate and the amount the recipient will receive. That rate is locked for 60 seconds while you review and confirm. If you confirm within the window you get that rate regardless of any movement afterwards. If the window expires, the app refreshes the rate and you review again. Card purchases are not rate locked; they convert when the merchant settles.

## Rate alerts

You can set a rate alert for any of the 20 supported currencies from the currency screen in the app. Choose the currency and the target rate, and Corella Bank sends a push notification when the mid market rate reaches that level. Alerts do not send a transfer automatically; they only notify you. You can hold up to ten active rate alerts at once.

## Supported currencies

Corella Bank supports 20 currencies for international transfers, including the United States dollar, the euro, the British pound, the New Zealand dollar, the Japanese yen, the Singapore dollar, the Indian rupee, the Philippine peso and the Canadian dollar. The full list is shown in the app. Card purchases can be made in any currency the card network supports, which is broader than the 20 transfer currencies.
