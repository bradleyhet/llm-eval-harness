# Fees and limits

This page lists every fee Corella Bank charges on personal accounts and the default transaction limits that apply. All amounts are in Australian dollars.

## Everyday account fees

The Everyday account has a monthly fee of $5, charged on the first day of each month for the previous month. The fee is waived for any month in which you deposit $1,000 or more into the account. Deposits from any source count, including salary, government payments, transfers from other banks and cash deposits at Corella Cash Network ATMs. Transfers from your own Saver account do not count towards the $1,000. There is no fee to open or close an Everyday account.

## Saver account fees

The Saver account has no monthly fee. There is no fee to open, hold or close a Saver, no fee to move money between your Saver and your Everyday account, and no minimum balance. The only thing that varies is the interest rate: you earn the bonus rate of 4.35% per year in months where you deposit at least $200 and make no withdrawals, and the base rate of 0.10% per year otherwise.

## Domestic transfer limits

The default daily limit for transfers to other Australian accounts is $10,000 per customer across all your accounts. You can raise this to $25,000 in the app with two factor approval, and the higher limit applies from the next calendar day. You can also lower the limit to any amount you choose. Transfers between your own Corella Bank accounts do not count against the limit. There is no fee for domestic transfers.

## International transfer limits

The default daily limit for international transfers is $5,000 per customer. It can be raised to $15,000, but only after a security call with the Corella Bank team, which you can request in the app. Each international transfer costs a flat $8 fee, and the currency conversion uses the mid market rate plus a margin of 0.5%. International transfers are not available while your account is on starter limits.

## Card and ATM limits

The Corella Card has a default daily purchase limit of $5,000 and a daily ATM withdrawal limit of $1,000, both counted across the physical and virtual card. Contactless purchases up to $200 do not need a PIN. Withdrawals at Corella Cash Network ATMs are free. Withdrawals at other Australian ATMs cost $2.50, and Corella Bank rebates up to three of those fees per calendar month. Overseas ATM withdrawals cost $5 each.

## Other fees

A paper statement posted to you costs $2.50 per statement; digital statements are free. A direct debit that is dishonoured because your account has insufficient funds incurs a $7.50 dishonour fee. Express card replacement by courier costs $15. A second or later damaged card replacement within 12 months costs $10. Recalling an international transfer after it has been processed costs $25. There are no overdrawn fees, no account keeping fees on the Saver and no fees for closing any account.
