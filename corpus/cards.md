# The Corella Card

Every Everyday account and Joint Everyday account comes with a Corella Card. The card is a debit card, so every purchase is paid from your account balance at the time of purchase.

## Virtual card

A virtual Corella Card is issued the moment your Everyday account is verified. The card number, expiry and security code are shown in the app and can be added to your phone wallet for tap payments straight away. The virtual card has its own number, separate from the physical card, so you can use it for online shopping and keep the physical card details private. If the virtual card number is compromised you can reissue it instantly in the app without affecting the physical card.

## Physical card

You can order a physical Corella Card from the card screen in the app. It is posted to your residential address, or to a nominated post office box if you have set one, and arrives within five to seven business days. The physical card is activated by tapping it against your phone in the app or by entering the last four digits of the card number. There is no fee for the first physical card.

## Setting and changing your PIN

Your card PIN is four digits. You choose it in the app when you order the physical card and can change it at any time from the card screen. The new PIN takes effect immediately for purchases with a PIN and for ATM withdrawals. Corella Bank never posts a PIN to you and never asks for it by phone or chat. If you forget your PIN you can view it in the app after unlocking with your passcode or biometrics.

## Purchase limits

The default daily purchase limit on a Corella Card is $5,000, counted across both the physical and virtual card. You can lower this limit in the app at any time and raise it back up to $5,000 with two factor approval. Contactless payments up to $200 do not require a PIN. Above $200 you will be asked to enter your PIN, or to unlock your phone if you are paying from your phone wallet.

## Using the card overseas

The Corella Card works in any country where the card network is accepted. Purchases in a foreign currency are converted at the mid market rate plus a margin of 0.5%. There is no separate international transaction fee on purchases, so tapping or paying with the card on holiday does not cost you anything extra beyond the currency margin. Overseas ATM withdrawals cost $5 each on top of the currency conversion. Overseas use is on by default, but you can turn it off in card controls and turn it back on before you travel.
