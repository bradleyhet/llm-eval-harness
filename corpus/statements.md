# Statements and records

Corella Bank keeps a full record of your transactions in the app and issues formal statements monthly. This page explains what is available and how to get it.

## Digital statements

A statement for each account is generated on the first day of every month covering the previous calendar month. Digital statements are free and are stored in the app under the documents screen for seven years. Each statement is a PDF and includes your name, address, BSB and account number, opening and closing balances, every transaction and any interest or fees. You can download or share a statement from the app at any time.

## Paper statements

If you need a statement posted to you, you can request a paper statement from the documents screen. Each paper statement costs $2.50, charged to your Everyday account, and is posted to your residential address or nominated post office box within five business days. Paper statements contain the same information as the digital version. You cannot subscribe to receive every statement on paper; each paper statement is requested individually.

## Exporting transactions

You can export transactions for any date range as a CSV or PDF file from the account screen. Exports are free and unlimited. The CSV export includes the date, description, amount, running balance, category and any note you added to the transaction, and is suitable for budgeting software or your accountant. Exports are generated instantly for ranges up to two years; longer ranges are delivered to the app's message centre within one business day.

## Interest summary

For Saver accounts, an annual interest summary is generated in the first week of July covering the financial year just ended. It shows the total interest paid on each Saver account and is stored under the documents screen alongside your monthly statements. The summary is free. Everyday accounts do not pay interest, so no interest summary is produced for them.

## Transaction notes and receipts

You can add a note and attach a photo of a receipt to any transaction in the app. Notes and receipts are kept with the transaction for as long as the transaction is visible, which is seven years, and are included in CSV exports. They are also useful when lodging a merchant dispute, because you can attach them as evidence directly from the transaction.
