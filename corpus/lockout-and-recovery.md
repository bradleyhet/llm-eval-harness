# Lockouts and account recovery

This page explains what happens when a PIN or passcode is entered incorrectly too many times, and how to regain access to your account.

## Card PIN lockout

If the wrong PIN is entered three times in a row at a terminal or ATM, the card is locked for PIN transactions. Contactless purchases under $200 continue to work while the card is locked, because they do not use the PIN. To unlock the card, open the card screen in the app and choose unlock PIN, which asks for two factor approval. You can also view or change your PIN there. The lock does not expire on its own; it stays until you unlock it in the app.

## App passcode lockout

If the wrong app passcode is entered five times in a row, the app is locked for 30 minutes. After the 30 minutes you can try again. If the wrong passcode is entered ten times in total without a successful sign in, the account is locked and the app shows a locked screen. To unlock an account locked this way you must call 1300 000 267 and complete identity verification with the team, who will then reset your passcode. Biometric attempts do not count towards the passcode lockout.

## Forgotten passcode

If you have forgotten your app passcode but can still unlock the app with your fingerprint or face, go to the security screen and choose reset passcode, then choose a new one. If biometrics are not set up, choose forgotten passcode on the sign in screen. You will be asked for a text message code and a fresh liveness selfie that is compared with your verified identity document. This takes a few minutes and your new passcode works immediately.

## Lost or replaced phone

If you lose your phone or get a new one, install the Corella app on the new phone and sign in with your mobile number. Because the new phone is not a trusted device, you will complete a text message code and a liveness selfie. Once verified, the new phone becomes your trusted device and the old phone is signed out automatically. If your mobile number has changed as well, call 1300 000 267 so the team can verify you and update the number.

## Suspected account takeover

If you see a transaction, payee or device you do not recognise, freeze your card immediately, remove any unknown device from the security screen, and call 1300 000 267. The line is open 24 hours a day for security issues. The team can lock the account, reverse pending transfers where possible, and start an investigation. Do not change your passcode from a device you think may be compromised.
