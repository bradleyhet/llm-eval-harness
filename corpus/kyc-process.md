# Identity verification process

This page explains what happens after you submit your identity documents, how long each step takes and what to do if verification fails.

## Automated verification

When you photograph your document and take the liveness selfie, the app checks the document's security features, compares the photo on the document with your selfie, and confirms the details against the issuing authority's records. This runs in the background and completes for most customers within about ten minutes. You can keep using the app while it runs, subject to starter limits, and you will receive a push notification when the result is in.

## Manual review

If any automated check cannot reach a confident result, your application goes to a verification specialist, who looks at your licence or passport photo personally. Manual review takes up to two business days, which is why it can seem to take a long time. Common reasons for manual review are glare on the document photo, a recent change of address, a name that differs slightly between documents, or a document type that cannot be checked electronically. You do not need to do anything during manual review unless the app asks you for a secondary document.

## If verification fails

If verification fails, the app tells you which check did not pass and lets you try again with a clearer photo or a different document. You can make three attempts. After three failed attempts, the application is paused and a specialist will contact you through in-app chat within one business day to arrange another way to verify. Corella Bank never asks you to send identity documents by text message or to a personal email address.

## Ongoing verification checks

Corella Bank periodically re-confirms customer details. If your primary document expires, for example your passport or licence ran out last month, the bank does care: the app will ask you to upload a current one within 60 days. If you do not, the account is placed on starter limits until you do. You may also be asked to re-verify if you sign in from a new device in a new country or if there is unusual account activity.

## Privacy of your documents

Document images are encrypted and stored for the period the law requires, and they are used only for identity verification and fraud prevention. They are never shown to support staff in chat. You can ask what personal information Corella Bank holds about you through the privacy request form in the app, and you will receive a response within 30 days.
