# Closing an account

You can close any Corella Bank account from the app. This page explains the steps for each account type and what happens to your money and records.

## Before you close

Cancel or move any direct debits and card subscriptions, because payments requested after closure will be dishonoured and the merchant may charge you a failed payment fee. Download any statements or exports you want, although statements remain available for 90 days after closure on request. Make sure any pending transfers or disputes have completed, because an account with a pending dispute or a pending international transfer cannot be closed until they resolve.

## Closing a sole account

To close an Everyday account in your own name, open the account settings and choose close account. The balance must be $0, or you nominate another Australian bank account to receive it. Your Corella Card is cancelled at the moment of closure. Closure is processed within two business days, and there is no fee. If it is your only Everyday account and you hold Saver accounts, the Savers must be closed first, because a Saver needs a linked Everyday account.

## Closing a joint account

Closing a Joint Everyday account needs both holders. One holder requests closure in the app and the other holder has seven days to approve it in their own app. If the second holder does not approve within seven days, the request lapses and the account stays open. When both have approved, the balance is transferred according to split instructions both holders confirm, and closure is processed within two business days. There is no fee.

## Closing a Saver account

A Saver account can be closed instantly from the account settings. The balance and any interest accrued up to the day of closure are paid into your linked Everyday account. There is no fee and no notice period. Closing a Saver part way through a month forfeits the bonus rate for that month, because the bonus requires no withdrawals in the month; the base rate of 0.10% applies to the closing month instead.

## After closure

You receive a final statement in the app on the first day of the following month. Statements for the closed account can be requested for 90 days after closure through in-app chat and are sent to the message centre. After 90 days, requests for records are handled through the privacy request form and take up to 30 days. Your app login remains active if you still hold any other Corella Bank account.

## Inactive accounts

If an Everyday or Saver account has no customer initiated transactions for 12 months, it is marked inactive and you are notified in the app and by text message. An inactive account still works normally; the marker is a prompt to check whether you still need it. If there are no customer initiated transactions for 24 months, the balance may be transferred to the government's unclaimed money scheme as the law requires, and the account is closed. You can reclaim unclaimed money through that scheme at any time.
