# Opening an account

Opening a Corella Bank account happens entirely in the Corella app and usually takes about five minutes from download to a working account.

## What you need before you start

Before you begin, have ready an Australian mobile number that can receive text messages, a residential address in Australia, and one of the accepted identity documents. You must be at least 16 years old and an Australian resident. You do not need an existing bank account, and there is no minimum opening deposit. If you plan to open a Joint Everyday account, both people need to complete their own individual sign up first.

## The sign up steps

Download the Corella app, enter your mobile number and confirm the six digit code sent by text. Enter your legal name, date of birth and residential address exactly as they appear on your identity document. Choose a six digit app passcode and, if your phone supports it, turn on fingerprint or face unlock. Photograph your identity document and take a short liveness selfie. Finally, choose whether you want an Everyday account, a Saver account or both. Most customers are verified within about ten minutes and can start using their account straight away.

## Starter limits before verification

If automated verification cannot confirm your identity immediately, your account opens with starter limits while a specialist reviews it. Under starter limits your total balance across all accounts is capped at $1,000, you cannot send international transfers, and your Corella Card cannot be ordered. Starter limits are lifted automatically once verification completes, which takes up to two business days. You will get a push notification when that happens.

## Getting your account details

Your BSB and account number are shown in the app under the account name as soon as verification is complete. You can share them from the app or copy them for your employer's payroll form. A virtual Corella Card is issued instantly for an Everyday account, and you can order a physical card at the same time. The physical card arrives in the post within five to seven business days.

## Adding a Saver later

You do not have to decide about a Saver when you sign up. You can add a Saver account at any time from the accounts screen in the app. It opens instantly with no further identity checks because it is linked to your verified Everyday account. You can hold up to five Saver accounts, each with its own name and savings goal.
