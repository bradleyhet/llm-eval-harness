# International transfers

You can send money overseas from your Everyday account in the app. This page covers who you can pay, what it costs, how long it takes and how to cancel.

## Where you can send money

Corella Bank sends international transfers to bank accounts in 48 countries in 20 currencies. The list of countries and currencies is shown in the app when you start a transfer. You need the recipient's full name, their address, and the bank details for their country, which the app prompts for, such as an account number and bank code or an international bank account number. Transfers to businesses are supported as long as the account is in the business's name.

## Fees and exchange rate

Every international transfer costs a flat fee of $8, deducted from your Everyday account in addition to the amount sent. The amount is converted at the mid market rate plus a margin of 0.5%. The rate you see when you confirm is locked for 60 seconds; if you take longer to confirm, the app refreshes the rate. The recipient's bank may charge its own receiving fee, which Corella Bank cannot control or see.

## Daily limit

The default daily limit for international transfers is $5,000 per customer. To raise it to $15,000 you request a security call in the app; a team member calls you on your registered mobile number, confirms your identity and the reason for the increase, and applies the higher limit from the next calendar day. The international limit is separate from the $10,000 domestic transfer limit. International transfers are not available on starter limits.

## Delivery time

International transfers usually arrive within one to three business days after you confirm. Transfers confirmed before 3pm Australian Eastern Standard Time on a business day are processed the same day. Transfers to some countries and currencies can take up to five business days because of local banking hours and public holidays. The app shows the expected arrival date before you confirm and updates the status as the transfer moves.

## Cancelling or recalling a transfer

You can cancel an international transfer for free from the transfer screen within 30 minutes of confirming, as long as it has not yet been processed. After processing, you can ask Corella Bank to recall the transfer. A recall costs $25, is not guaranteed, and depends on the recipient's bank agreeing to return the funds. If the recall succeeds, the money comes back at the exchange rate on the day of return, which may differ from the rate you sent at.
