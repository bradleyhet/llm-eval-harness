# Chargeback timelines

A chargeback is the process by which Corella Bank recovers money from a merchant's bank on your behalf after a dispute. This page explains the stages and how long each one takes.

## Initial review

After you lodge a dispute, a disputes specialist reviews it within two business days. At this stage they confirm the dispute is within the 60 day lodgement window, that the evidence supports a chargeback under the card network's rules, and whether provisional credit applies. You receive a message in the app when the review is done, telling you whether the chargeback has been raised or whether more information is needed.

## Merchant response window

Once the chargeback is raised, the merchant's bank has 30 days to respond. The merchant can accept the chargeback, in which case the money is returned and the dispute closes, or they can provide evidence that the transaction was valid. Most merchants respond within the first two weeks. You cannot speed up this window, but you can add evidence to your dispute at any time and the specialist will forward it.

## Resolution window

The whole chargeback process, from lodgement to final decision, takes up to 45 business days. Most disputes are resolved well inside that, and simple unauthorised transaction disputes are often closed within three weeks. If the merchant contests the chargeback with evidence, the specialist reviews their response and may ask you for a reply within seven days. The 45 business day resolution window is the outer limit; if a case is going to exceed it you will be told why.

## Outcomes

If the chargeback is upheld, the disputed amount stays in your account or is credited if provisional credit was not paid. If the chargeback is refused, any provisional credit is reversed and the app shows the merchant's evidence and the reason for the decision. A refused dispute can be escalated to the Corella Bank complaints team from the dispute screen within 30 days of the decision.

## Escalating a refused dispute

The complaints team looks at the dispute afresh and responds within 30 days. If you are still unhappy after the complaints team's decision, the app gives you the details of the external dispute resolution scheme that Corella Bank belongs to, which is free to use. Escalating a dispute does not affect your account or your card, and you can keep using both while the escalation is considered.
